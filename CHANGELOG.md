# Changelog

## 0.5.0

### Added

- Floating damage indicators for damage dealt to enemies, with a red number, gold outline, smooth upward drift, and quick fade.
- Commands to view and set the threat radar chevron, damage number, and damage outline colors using RGB hex.
- Pale oak log and plank stonecutting recipes for planks, slabs, stairs, fences, fence gates, doors, trapdoors, signs, hanging signs, buttons, and pressure plates.

### Improved

- Threat radar chevrons use smoother strokes with rounded, antialiased ends.
- Radar markers now fade out cleanly at the configured range instead of reappearing beyond it.
- Breadcrumb markers and connecting lines are occluded by world blocks.
